Casting a Write-In Vote

Your ballot will include a write-in line for County Commissioner. Here is how to make your vote count:

  1. Find the Spokane County Commissioner, District 4 race on your ballot.
  2. Select the write-in option by filling in the bubble or oval next to the blank line.
  3. On the blank line, write Rachelle Miller.
  4. Return your ballot by mail or drop it at any official Spokane County drop box by 8 PM on August 4.

Primary Election · August 4 · Spokane County

Write-In Voting Instructions

Three steps. That is all it takes.

Write-in votes in Washington State are fully counted when properly cast. It takes one extra step and it matters.

01

Find the race

Look for Spokane County Commissioner, District 4 on your ballot.

02

Mark the write-in bubble

Fill in the oval next to the write-in line. This step is required or your vote will not count.

03

Write Rachelle's name

On the blank line, write Rachelle Miller. Spelling matters. Write it exactly as shown.

Washington State ballots are mailed to all registered voters. Return by mail (postmarked by August 4) or drop at a Spokane County ballot drop box by 8 PM on August 4.

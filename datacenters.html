The Fight

No buildout for billionaires.

The Spokane City Council recently passed an emergency moratorium on new large data centers within city limits. The reason: Avista, the utility that powers Eastern Washington, received an approach from an unnamed corporation seeking up to 500 megawatts of electricity, equal to half the power used by every home and business in Spokane County. That is not a rumor. It is a matter of public record, disclosed to federal securities regulators. The city now has a one year pause to figure out what it wants to do. The county has no such protection. The County Commissioner controls zoning, permitting, and tax abatements in unincorporated Spokane County, and that is exactly where a corporation blocked from the city will look next. Rachelle Miller is running to make sure Spokane County residents have a voice before that happens.

Hyperscale tech corporations are spending billions to plant data centers across the country and Spokane County is in their sights. They want our land. They need our water. They need our power. Plus tax breaks while they take it. Promised jobs never materialize. The utility bills go up. The water table goes down. We pay. They profit.

The corporations have lawyers and lobbyists. Spokane County needs someone at the table who works for us. The County Commissioner has real authority over zoning, land use, permitting, water use, and tax abatements. Rachelle will use every tool available to make sure residents have a voice before approvals are granted, not after.

Zero meaningful local jobs created in exchange for what they take from our community
Your say on zoning, permitting, and tax abatements belongs to Spokane County residents, not billionaires and Big Tech

Write in Rachelle Miller on August 4. It is the only way Spokane County gets a voice at the table.

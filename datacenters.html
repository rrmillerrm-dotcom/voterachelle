Unregistered Developer Discussions

County leaders held secret discussions for over a year.

[span_23](start_span)

Medical Lake Highway[span_23](end_span). [span_24](start_span)The proposed facility could eventually use up to 1,000 megawatts of power, nearly the entire output of Avista's Washington and Idaho service area combined[span_24](end_span).

The West Plains

A community already fighting for clean water.

[span_30](start_span)

The West Plains is not an abstract location on a map[span_30](end_span). [span_31](start_span)It is a community of families, farms, and small operations whose well water has been contaminated with PFAS, so-called forever chemicals, from firefighting foam used at Fairchild Air Force Base and Spokane International Airport[span_31](end_span). [span_32](start_span)The EPA tested 411 private wells in the area and found that 57% contained PFAS levels exceeding safe drinking water standards[span_32](end_span). [span_33](start_span)Residents have been dealing with this crisis for years[span_33](end_span).

[span_34](start_span)

County officials claim data centers on the West Plains would use wastewater rather than aquifer water[span_34](end_span). [span_35](start_span)The people of the West Plains, who have already had their water contaminated once while officials downplayed their concerns, may reasonably ask for more than an official's word on that[span_35](end_span).

[span_36](start_span)

The county currently has no specific regulations covering data centers, as confirmed by the county's own planning director[span_36](end_span).

Source: KXLY Reporting · KHQ Reporting · Spokesman-Review Reporting

Proposals for a moratorium blocked.

Our Farms

Data centers are water competitors, not neighbors.

[span_45](start_span)

Spokane County's agricultural community depends on the same aquifer, the same power grid, and the same unincorporated land that hyperscale AI data centers are targeting[span_45](end_span). [span_46](start_span)For farmers and ranchers on the West Plains and across the county, the impacts are immediate and permanent[span_46](end_span).

[span_47](start_span)

A single hyperscale facility can consume millions of gallons daily through cooling systems, drawing from the same Spokane Valley-Rathdrum Prairie Aquifer that farmers depend on for irrigation and livestock[span_47](end_span). [span_48](start_span)When the water table drops, small producers lose first[span_48](end_span).

[span_49](start_span)

Utility rate increases driven by data center demand do not hit a billion-dollar tech company the way they hit a small dairy or vegetable operation running pumps, refrigeration, and equipment year-round[span_49](end_span). [span_50](start_span)Tight margins get tighter[span_50](end_span). [span_51](start_span)Promised construction jobs come and go, leaving minimal permanent employment and a maximum permanent draw on shared resources[span_51](end_span).

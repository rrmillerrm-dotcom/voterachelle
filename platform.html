What I am running on.

Accountable government. Honest answers. Fighting for Spokane County residents.

On the Issues

No handouts for Big Tech. Protect our water, land, and power.

While the public was learning that an unnamed corporation had approached Avista seeking up to 500 megawatts of power, county leaders had already been meeting privately for over a year regarding proposals to build massive AI data centers on the West Plains. Facilities of this scale could eventually demand up to 1,000 megawatts—nearly Avista's entire regional generating capacity. Crucial details were uncovered not through proactive county announcements, but by local residents filing public records requests.

The West Plains is a community already navigating PFAS water contamination across private wells. Yet, Spokane County currently lacks specific regulations to govern hyperscale data centers. Despite calls for caution, requests for a moratorium were turned down while competing candidates have remained silent.

As County Commissioner, Rachelle will push for a moratorium to allow full environmental and public impact assessments, demand complete transparency on developer negotiations, and use every local zoning, permitting, and tax policy tool to ensure residents have a real voice before decisions are finalized.

On the Issues

Wildfire protection for Spokane County.

Spokane County is hurting. As our community recovers, this section will outline Rachelle's full plan for proactive wildfire prevention, emergency response coordination, and commission-led land management to protect local homes and natural resources.

On the Issues

Affordable housing & sensible growth.

Spokane County families are facing severe housing affordability challenges. The County Commission directly controls unincorporated zoning and oversees housing trust funds. Rachelle will ensure these policy tools are deployed transparently to expand housing opportunities while protecting existing neighborhoods and rural resources.

On the Issues

Jail reform & indigent defense mandates.

Spokane County's jail facility is overcrowded and outdated, while the local public defense system faces massive funding gaps. The State of Washington covers only 4% of indigent defense costs while repeatedly issuing unfunded judicial mandates. New court-ordered defense standards will add an estimated $15 million annually to county expenses—shifting state obligations directly onto local taxpayers.

Rachelle brings 20 years of experience navigating complex public systems. She will demand honest accounting of county justice spending and actively push state leaders in Olympia to fully fund the mandates they create.

On the Issues

Addressing the opioid crisis & mental health.

Spokane County has seen real progress by directing opioid settlement dollars into frontline intervention. The Regional Stabilization Center has assisted thousands of individuals, and co-responder models are reducing emergency room visits and jail admissions. Dedicated facilities like the P.A.T.H. Center represent vital steps forward when properly maintained.

However, funding streams remain fragile. As commissioner, Rachelle will work to secure stable funding for proven recovery programs, ensuring resources directly reach individuals and families rather than getting tied up in administrative overhead.

On the Issues

Fiscal accountability & budget transparency.

Spokane County faces significant budget deficits—not because residents aren't paying their fair share, but because state mandates continually outpace local revenue growth capped at 1% annually. Adding more taxes onto working families is not the solution.

Rachelle will prioritize strict fiscal oversight: auditing existing revenue streams, directing mental health sales tax and settlement funds straight to frontline services, and holding Olympia accountable for dumping unfunded state mandates onto local property owners.
